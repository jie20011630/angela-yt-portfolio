Project Overview

Mic Check 1,2 is a short interactive narrative game that puts players in the mind of Seunghyuk, a K-pop idol on the edge of debut. Through dialogue choices, time-sensitive QTEs, and shifting visual cues, players experience how anxiety, performance, and identity intertwine under the spotlight.

Character & Worldbuilding

The WY Entertainment Tower

Set in a heightened version of present-day Seoul, Mic Check 1,2 unfolds within the glass-and-steel monolith of WY Entertainment Tower — a vertical empire where fame is both currency and confinement.

Every floor represents a step in the idol hierarchy: noisy trainee dorms at the bottom, media control offices in the middle, and the mythic “perfect debut” stage rumored to sit at the top. The higher you climb, the colder it gets.

The Tower is both a stage and a prison — a place where light exposes as much as it blinds.

SEUNGHYUK (승혁)

Main Character / Player’s Perspective

  • Lead vocalist of the K-pop group LUMEN.
  • Quiet, disciplined, and adored for his flawless image.
  • Secretly struggles with anxiety and panic attacks hidden beneath perfection.
  • Player choices reveal how he balances ambition, authenticity, and emotional survival.

JAEWON (재원)

Narrative Foil / Emotional Catalyst

  • Former best friend and creative partner to Seunghyuk.
  • Now the charismatic leader of rival group X1NDR.
  • Fueled by pride and unresolved tension from their shared past.
  • Represents the path Seunghyuk could have taken — expressive, volatile, and unrestrained.

The game begins with Seunghyuk’s long-awaited dome debut ending in a sudden collapse on stage. Time rewinds, allowing players to relive his trainee years and the choices that shaped his career.

Each decision affects Fame, Trust, and Anxiety, altering how others perceive him—and how he perceives himself.

Players can guide Seunghyuk toward different outcomes:

Reclaiming the spotlight as a solo artist.
Returning to LUMEN to rebuild connection and trust.

Every route explores a different version of the same question: What does it truly mean to be seen?

Visual Concept

All character portraits and CG backgrounds in Mic Check 1,2 were AI-generated using ChatGPT’s DALL·E. The visual direction focused on achieving a consistent tone between stylized realism and narrative mood.

I refined each prompt iteratively, adjusting keywords, composition, and lighting cues to produce coherent visual assets that matched the story’s psychological atmosphere. This process became an experiment in human–AI co-creation, where language precision shaped visual emotion.

SeungHyuk’s style is minimalist and effortlessly stylish, with a preference for neutral tones like black, gray, and beige. He often wears oversized sweaters, relaxed shirts, and tailored trousers, layered with light jackets. His signature look includes transparent square-framed glasses, silver rings, a thin chain, and a classic watch — reflecting his quiet, refined personality.

A sleek, futuristic K-pop dome setup with a central lift, massive LED panels, glowing spotlights, and an audience holding lightsticks.

Core Experience

Design Layers

  1. 01

    Narrative Choices

    • Branching dialogue exposes Seunghyuk’s inner fears, ambitions, and fragile connections with others.
    • Decisions drive the balance between ambition, authenticity, and emotional survival.
  2. 02

    Status System

    • Physical Health tracks burnout; dropping to zero ends the game.
    • Mental Health reflects emotional stability and recovery.
    • Relationships measure trust and empathy with key characters.
  3. 03

    Panic QTEs

    • Time-sensitive interactions simulate anxiety spikes during performances.
    • Steady timing from the player keeps panic under control.
  4. 04

    Sensory Design

    • Audio distortion, visual blur, and off-beat timing mirror panic attacks.
    • Atmospheric cues reinforce the tension of living on the edge of control.

Process Highlight

II

UI Prototype

Designed minimalist dialogue and status interfaces that visually echo Seunghyuk’s tension.

III

QTE Timing Test

Tuned reaction windows to capture panic without punishing the player.

IV

Sound Integration

🔊 Heartbeat QTE — layered pulse rhythm to mimic the focus and disorientation during panic.

🔊 Collapse Scene — faded-in score after blackout to amplify the feeling of tragedy and loss.

PV & Content Creation

I directed and animated a short PV to extend Mic Check 1,2’s atmosphere into motion. Using cinematic cuts, layered text, and symbolic transitions, the video visualizes Seunghyuk’s fractured perception between stage and self—turning rhythm, breath, and silence into emotional language.

Scene 2 · Eye Blink Transition

Scene 3 · Character Introduction

Reflection

Learnings

  • Learned how to translate psychological states—like anxiety and pressure—into interactive mechanics through pacing, sound, and timing.
  • Refined my ability to write branching narratives that balance player agency with emotional focus.
  • Gained hands-on experience in AI-assisted visual generation, understanding how precise language and iteration shape artistic coherence.

Next Steps

  • Expand story arcs to deepen relationships and reveal more perspectives within the idol system.
  • Introduce additional branching endings for greater emotional and narrative replay value.
  • Enhance the status system and QTE feedback for better accessibility and smoother difficulty balance.
